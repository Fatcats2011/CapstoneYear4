# Ghost animations: design

2026-11-19 · branch `steam-phase1a` · status: parts 1–2 built (78 versions, 54 hand clips), not yet triggered in game

## Why
The ghosts have only six clips (idle float, player-select idle, grab, hooray, clap, lose). They show little personality during a match, and the lobby, countdown and results reuse the same few poses. We want a set of short, characterful animations across the whole game, chosen at random from several versions, that read clearly even when the player only sees the ghost from behind.

## Goals
- 25 actions, each with 2–4 versions. A version is picked at random and never repeats twice running.
- Every version reads **from behind**, from the player camera, in under a second.
- They play over the driving pose, so the ghost keeps steering and leaning while it emotes.
- One shared system used by the match, lobby, countdown, results and title skits.

## Non-goals
- No new model, rig or retopology. Everything uses the existing bones.
- No change to gameplay, controls, timings or rules. Animations are cosmetic only.
- No new sounds in this spec. Any sound comes later, from CC0 online sources, with the user's OK.

## Anti-goals
- Animations that only read from the front (faces, eyes, small hand gestures in front of the body).
- Animations that block the player's view or clutter four-player split-screen.
- Spam: the same animation firing again and again in a busy moment.

## Readability rules (from behind)
1. **Use the silhouette, not the face.** Arms go above the head or out wide, the body leans hard or pops up out of the seat, and the hat and sheet tail exaggerate the motion.
2. **Use the head spin.** A ghost can turn its head 180° to look back at the camera. That gives a face from behind, and it is the signature move.
3. **Exaggerate.** Each one has to read at a glance from a scooter length away.
4. **Short.** Most last 0.6–1.5 s while driving. Results and lobby animations can run longer (up to ~2.5 s, like the podium acts).
5. **The scooter hides the lower body.** Assume only the head, hands, hat and tail are seen above the seat. *To confirm on the real player camera.*
6. **From behind, the body hides both hands** in the driving pose (checked in Blender, 2026-11-19). Only the horns and tail show. A hand move only reads if the hands go above the horns or out past the body's sides.
7. **The "head spin" is a body turn.** Turning only the `Head` bone 180° tears the mesh (only ~15% of the body skin follows the head, while the eyes and horns follow it fully). A head turn of up to 60–90° only shows one eye. What reads is the whole ghost turning in its seat: `Torso` about 100° plus `Head` about 60°, which shows the full face cleanly.
8. **The scooter seat hides the tail from the player camera** (in-game capture, 2026-11-19). Moves carried by the tail alone (10b Bum Wiggle, 22b Tail Waggle) look alike and barely read from behind. Rework them with the hands or body in part 2. The ghost is also small at the real camera distance (about 15% of frame height), so every move needs big numbers: the first-guess values were 2–3× too small.

## The rig (as it is)
`Official_Anim_4.fbx`, `Armature.001`:
- `Torso` → `Head` (the hat sits on the head)
- `Torso` → `Hand.Left` → `Finger.Left`, and `Torso` → `Hand.Right`. Floating hands, no arms.
- `Tail1` → `Tail2`, and `Torso` → `TailIK` → `Tail3`

Clips today, in `Assets/Animations/Character Animations/`: Driving blend tree (Idle Float / Idle Player Select), Grab Left, End Screen Hooray, End Screen Clapping, End Screen Lose. They live on one Base Layer in `PlayerAnimator.controller`, chosen by the `End Status` int, the `Speed` float and the `StealLeft` trigger.

## How they're built
Each version uses one or more of these:

| Key | Method | What it covers | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Code** | Whole-body movement in code: lift, lean, nose up/down, yaw spin, squash and stretch. Applied to `Torso` after the Animator runs, in the same style as `PodiumActs` | Hops, spins, lunges, bounces, sinking, falling | No art |
| **Bone** | Single-bone tricks in code: head spin, hat pop or flip, tail flare, fade (material transparency) | Head spins, hat tricks, tail whips, fades | No art |
| **Clip** | New hand animation clip, keyed on the two hand bones (and the head where needed) | Every arm pose: fists, waves, claps, holding things up | A new `.anim` |

Clips are generated by an editor builder script from keyframe tables (like the other `DoA/Setup/...` menu items), so they can be tuned and rebuilt, then polished by hand in the Animation window if wanted.

## System

### Animator
- A new **upper-body layer** in `PlayerAnimator.controller`, masked to `Head`, `Hand.Left`, `Finger.Left` and `Hand.Right`, set to override.
- It holds one state per clip version and is driven by a trigger plus an int saying which clip.
- The Base Layer keeps the driving blend tree, so steering lean carries on underneath.

### `GhostEmotes` (one component on the ghost)
- `Play(GhostAction action)` picks a version from that action's shuffled list (reusing `TitlePlaylist`, so it never repeats twice running). It then fires the clip and/or runs the Code and Bone parts.
- **Code** and **Bone** parts run in `LateUpdate`, after the Animator, as offsets on top of whatever the Animator set. They always end back at rest, like `PodiumActs`.
- **Priorities:** a new animation only interrupts a lower-priority one. Suggested order: Robbed > Steal > Hit > Jump Scare > Delivery > Airtime > Boost > Golden > Emotes > Side-Eye > Scheme > Bored.
- **Cooldowns:** each action has a per-ghost cooldown (suggested 3–5 s; the Bored one longer). Emotes from the d-pad skip the cooldown but can't stack.
- The same component is used by the player avatar, `TitleActor` and the results podium. `PodiumPerformer` can call it for results animations alongside its existing acts.

### Triggers
| Action | Fires from |
|---|---|
| Cackle / Fist Shake | The steal (the thief and the victim): where `StealLeft` fires today in `OrderHandler` |
| Delivery Flourish | A completed delivery |
| Hold Your Hat | Boost used |
| Wheee | Scooter off the ground for longer than a threshold |
| Seeing Stars | Hit or crash above an impact threshold |
| Treasure Hold | Picking up the golden parcel, then now and then while carrying it |
| Jump Scare | Near miss, or a hunter bot / rival close behind |
| Bored | Same idle timer as the boost idle hint |
| Taunt / Big Wave | The d-pad emotes (alongside the existing emote bubble) |
| Hat Show-Off | Hat or colour change in customisation |
| Materialise | Player joins the lobby |
| Warm Up | Player readies up |
| Rev Bounce | Countdown beeps and "Go" |
| Victory / Tantrum / Fade Away | Results podium, by finishing place |
| Scheme | Closing in on a rival who is carrying a parcel (distance and closing speed) |
| Side-Eye | Carrying a parcel with a rival close behind (shares Jump Scare's "close behind" check, at a longer range) |
| Impatient | Readied up in the lobby while others aren't, after a few seconds |
| Raspberry | A d-pad emote (which direction, and what it replaces, is an open question) |
| Mirror Check | Hat or colour change in customisation (shares a playlist with Hat Show-Off) |
| Knuckle Crack | Readying up (shares a playlist with Warm Up), or the first countdown beep |
| Crown Polish | Winner on the podium (joins Victory's playlist) |

*Each trigger source still has to be confirmed in code. Airtime, near miss and impact thresholds may not exist yet.*

### Online
- Match animations fire from events every machine already sees (steal, delivery, boost cue, hits), so each machine plays them locally. Versions are picked per machine. They're cosmetic, so they don't need to match.
- **The d-pad emotes may not be sent online.** No emote code was found in `Assets/Scripts/Online`. Taunt and Big Wave need checking, and probably a small online message, so other players see them.

## Review: does it read from behind?
- A capture scene puts a camera exactly where the player camera sits, then plays every version in turn and records it (the same way `TitleScreenCapture` works).
- Repeat with a four-player split-screen camera.
- Any version that doesn't read at a glance gets bigger or gets cut.

## Build order
1. `GhostEmotes`, the upper-body layer and the clip builder script.
2. The Code and Bone versions, which prove the pipeline with no new art.
3. Hand clips.
4. Hooking up the triggers in the match, lobby, countdown and results.
5. Online emote check and fix.
6. The from-behind capture, then review and cut.
7. GDD (what the player sees, when each plays) and TDD (`GhostEmotes`, the layer, the builder, tests) updates. Each gets a Revision History line.

## Tests (edit mode)
- The version picker never repeats twice running and covers every version.
- Priorities: a higher action interrupts a lower one, and never the reverse.
- Cooldowns hold.
- Every Code and Bone part starts and ends at rest (like the existing `PodiumActs` tests).
- The clip builder makes every clip it lists, with only the masked bones keyed.

## Playtest fixes (2026-11-20)
- **26. Wall Bump** (new, last in `GhostAction`): the scooter hits a wall at 10 m/s or more (`BallCollision`, on the machine that drives it). Two short code-only reactions, *Wobble* (1.0 s: knocked back, then a dying side-to-side sway, the head a beat behind) and *Knocked Silly* (1.2 s: a squash and a hat hop, then a bobblehead wobble). They play at 50% size at 10 m/s, full size from 30 m/s. Priority 55 (over Wheee, under Delivery), cooldown 1.5 s. Online it goes out as `CueKind.WallBump` with the speed in whole m/s, only when it played on the sender. Reactions are a new length category, 0.8–1.2 s; emotes stay 1.5–2.5 s.
- **Landing:** a Wheee still playing when the ghost lands eases back to rest over 0.25 s (`GhostEmotes.EaseOut`) instead of playing on.
- **Drop In** falls with gravity (speeding up into the ground), then squashes and makes a small rebound hop.
- **Eyes after a fade:** a colour change during a fade (`Renderer.materials` copies every slot) left the eye and horn slots wearing the body's dithering material. The cover now comes off by slot index, and `ScooterLook.ShowColour` and `PhaseIndicator` write `sharedMaterials`.
- **Parcel:** held at 85% of its rack size; its arc over the head now clears by the parcel's own half height.
- **Polish:** smooth hops (`Hops`) instead of `abs(sin)` bounces; slower Shoulder Shake (2.0 s, 4 hops), Neck Crack (2.0 s), Hat Flip (1.8 s, with a toss and a catch), and half the bob rate in Drum Solo, Arm Pump Dance and Fist Pound; swings build and die away; secondary motion (head lag, settle) in Crown Overhead, Polish, Nap and Big Stretch. Hand clips unchanged.
- 26 actions, 80 versions.
