# Gold Rush: making it feel unique (brainstorm, 2026-11-04)

**Decided 2026-11-05: idea 1, the Mother Lode, is built, and the drain is gone. See docs/game-modes-decisions.md.**

Playtest note: "Mode isn't very unique in current state." At the time, the mode was 3x the packages, a score drain when you stop delivering, and double steals in the last 30 seconds. From behind the wheel, that plays like Standard with more clutter.

The problem to solve: Gold Rush needs one thing a player would describe to a friend. Each idea below tries to be that one thing.

## Ideas

**1. The Mother Lode (recommended)**
A single huge gold package spawns every 30 to 40 seconds, announced with a beacon and on every compass. Whoever delivers it gets a big payout, and everyone fights over it. It is slow to carry, and it spills small gold pickups behind the carrier so chasers get paid too.
- Why: creates a repeating "everyone converge" moment, which Standard only has once, at the very end.
- Reuses: the crown's beacon and compass marker, the steal rules, the golden order's look.

**2. Gold fever**
Each delivery in a row raises your multiplier (x2, x3, x4). Being stolen from or falling in water resets it. Leaders glow brighter as their multiplier climbs, so they become targets.
- Why: replaces the drain's punishment with something to protect and something to hunt.
- Reuses: the purple flame treatment, scoreboard rows.

**3. The vein moves**
Only one neighbourhood is "rich" at a time, paying triple. It moves every 30 seconds, marked on the compass. Everyone migrates together, so the whole map gets used and players keep colliding.
- Why: changes where you drive, which is what makes a mode feel different.

**4. Dropped gold**
Gets hit and your money physically spills on the road as pickups anyone can grab. Your score isn't safe until it's banked at a delivery.
- Why: very readable, very funny, and it makes every boost hit dramatic.
- Cost: needs pickups synced online, the largest build of these four.

**5. Claim jumpers**
Delivering claims that drop-off for you for 20 seconds: only you get paid there, and rivals who deliver to it pay you a cut.
- Why: adds territory, but it's the hardest to read at a glance. Probably one to skip.

## What I'd cut either way

The drain is the least fun part. It punishes the player without giving anyone a moment to react to, so it should go if any of ideas 1, 2 or 4 is picked.

## Recommendation

Build idea 1, and keep double steals in the last 30 seconds. If it still feels thin after a playtest, add idea 2.
